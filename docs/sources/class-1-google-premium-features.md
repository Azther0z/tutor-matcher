# Premium Meet features for Google Workspace

Your Google Workspace Edition or Google One subscription determines your access to different Google Meet features. Find out what editions have access to which Premium Google Meet features.

- [Maximum call length](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/7317473#length-limits) - Users can have one-on-one calls and group calls for up to 24 hours.
- [Multiple Co-Hosts](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/10885841) - Meeting hosts can add up to 25 co-hosts to their meetings.
