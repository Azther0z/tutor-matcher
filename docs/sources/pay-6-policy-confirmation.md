Question: How long should lesson earnings be held before they enter the Tutor's available wallet balance?

Answer: 24 hours.

Question: When should the hold period start?

Answer: At the lesson's scheduled end. The Tutor must still confirm the lesson as completed before earnings can clear.

Question: If an Admin approves a refund after lesson earnings have cleared, who is responsible for the amount already credited to the Tutor?

Answer: The platform is responsible. Return the full amount to the Student and do not take the Tutor's cleared earning back, even if the Tutor has withdrawn it.

Question: What happens when a refund request is still pending when the hold period ends?

Answer: Keep the earning pending until the Admin decides. If the request is rejected, keep the original clearing deadline. If approved before clearing, do not credit lesson earnings.

Question: Should eligible completed lessons from before PAY-6 be cleared?

Answer: Yes. Include existing lessons that meet the same rules.

Question: When does settlement run?

Answer: Before the Tutor's wallet balance, summary or transaction history is read, and before the wallet is used to pay for a lesson or request a payout. Opening a booking detail does not run settlement. Do not add a scheduler or manual settlement action.

Question: What should the Tutor see while earnings are pending?

Answer: Show when the earning becomes eligible to clear and whether it is waiting for Tutor completion, the 24-hour period or an Admin's refund decision. Say that it moves to the wallet the next time the wallet is viewed or used.
