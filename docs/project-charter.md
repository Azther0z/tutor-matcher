# Project Charter — Tutor Matcher

## Overview

Tutor Matcher is a matchmaking platform that connects tutors and students whose needs align in subject, schedule, and price. Students discover and book tutors; tutors manage their classes, availability, and promotional content; admins oversee disputes and platform integrity.

## Problem Statement

Finding a tutor who matches a student's required subject, preferred schedule, and budget is currently fragmented and manual. Tutor Matcher centralises this process into a single platform with transparent search, slot-based booking, and verified payment tracking.

## Goals

1. Enable tutors to manage classes, 30-minute availability slots, pricing, and promotional posts.
2. Enable students to search tutors by subject, price, and review score; book available slots; submit payment proof; and leave reviews after completed lessons.
3. Provide admins with tools to investigate and resolve user reports and disputes.
4. Ensure payment transparency through transfer-proof submission and status tracking.

## User Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Tutor** | Creates classes, sets hourly prices, declares availability, writes posts, accepts/cancels bookings |
| **Student** | Searches classes, books slots, submits payment proof, writes reviews |
| **Admin** | Reviews reports, responds to disputes, manages platform issues |

## Scope

**In scope:** Member registration and profiles, class and availability management, booking lifecycle, payment proof submission, review system, report and dispute handling, tutor promotional posts.

**Out of scope:** Real-time video/audio sessions, automated payment processing, mobile native apps (initial release).
